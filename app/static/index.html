Welcome to your workspace.

Enter your private owner key to load your emails. Your key is never saved in browser storage.

Where do I find my owner key?

Your Vercel project uses APP_API_KEY. If the generated secret isn’t available to you, replace it with your own random key of at least 32 characters in Vercel’s environment settings and redeploy. Never share it in chat.
